    What this leaves out
    • Writing the lessons. The 642 lessons were written once, by Claude sessions, and are shared by every student. That cost doesn't grow with students, but it isn't free: it is your time and plan usage.
    • Payment fees, taxes, sales and support staff, and anything a school asks for (training, integration).
    • Free tier: ₹0 on this page means list price, not a bill. The free tier also lets Google use the data to improve its products, so use a paid key for children's data. Ask your school and a lawyer.
    • Prices move: Google's gemini-3.8-flash is half price until 2026-12-31 and doubles from 2027-01-01.
    • The token counts come from few runs (6 stories, 4 plans, 1 coaching run). Treat the AI figure as the right size, not exact. Run scripts\pilot_report.py after the pilot for measured cost per student.
