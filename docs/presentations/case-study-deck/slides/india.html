Aarav is not a niche. In 2025-26, 2.89 crore students are enrolled in Class 11 and 12 in India. In 2025, 22.8 lakh registered for NEET UG and 15.4 lakh for JEE Main, about 38 lakh registrations, though some students take both, and every one of them is examined in physics. And almost 90 percent of 14 to 16 year olds in rural India have a smartphone at home, which is all EcoLearn needs: it runs in a browser.
