Can students reach it?

The phone is already in the house

14-16 year olds in rural India

Can use a smartphone · 82.2%

Boys who own one · 36.2%

Girls who own one · 26.9%

EcoLearn runs in a browser on the family phone: no app, no download.

Source: ASER 2024 (Rural), 6,49,491 children in 605 districts; self-reported

Can students actually reach a product like this? The ASER 2024 survey of rural India found that about 90 percent of 14 to 16 year olds have a smartphone at home, and 82 percent know how to use one. Owning one is rarer: 36 percent of boys and 27 percent of girls. That is why EcoLearn runs in a browser on whatever phone the family has, with no app to install. And that is rural India; urban access is higher.
