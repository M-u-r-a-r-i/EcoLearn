Step five. After the lesson there is one multiple-choice question, again in cricket: a fielder runs 25 metres in and 10 back, what distance did she cover. Before he checks, Aarav says how sure he is. That matters: a wrong answer he was sure about is a firmly held misconception, the kind worth a minute of class time. The line “Before you pick, read each option once more” is our pre-registered pilot experiment: a random half of each class sees it, the other half doesn’t, and we compare first-try accuracy. Grading uses no AI.
