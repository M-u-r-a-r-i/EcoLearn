Simulated data: our demo class, generated by script and run through the real app. Not pilot results.

The headline measure. When a class starts, every student takes the starting check. When the teacher decides, they open the end check: the same eight ideas, but different questions, and the two forms are swapped between halves of the class so one form being easier cancels out. In this simulated class, right answers go from 56 to 74 percent, over 168 paired answers from 21 students. Mix-ups chosen fall from 60 to 35. And we show the honest detail too: 35 mix-ups fixed, 10 ideas learned, but 16 answers slipped back from right to wrong. In the real pilot, these are the numbers we will report.
