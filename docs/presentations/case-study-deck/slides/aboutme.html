Step nine. Many apps profile students silently. EcoLearn shows Aarav what it has concluded about him, why, and what it changes. Here: sometimes you’re sure, but it’s a mix-up, 4 of 11 answers you were sure about were wrong, so you get a short reminder to check before you pick, and those ideas come back sooner. And from his survey: you like to start by trying, so “try an example” comes first. If anything isn’t him, one tap on “That’s not me” switches it off. His behaviour can also correct what he told us: if he said he’s a fast reader but skims, lessons go step by step.
