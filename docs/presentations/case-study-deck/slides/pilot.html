So how do we get from a simulated class to real evidence? Our pilot is planned, randomised, and written down before it starts. Each new student is assigned to one of two arms, balanced within their class; one arm sees the “Before you pick” prompt on every check, the other doesn’t. The one primary outcome, first-try accuracy on lesson checks, was fixed in advance, so we can’t pick a flattering number afterwards. We will report the difference with an uncertainty range, alongside the before and after you just saw. Everything on the last five slides is produced by the product today; the pilot simply puts real students in place of the simulated class.
