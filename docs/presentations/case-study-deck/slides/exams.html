Students examined in physics, 2025

Three exams, lakhs of physics students each

Physics is in every one of these exams. The groups overlap, so don’t add them up.

CBSE alone is about 17 lakh Class 12 students; ISC and the state boards add many more.

Sources: NTA, NEET UG 2025 (22.76 lakh) and JEE Main 2025 notice (15,39,848 unique); CBSE Class 12 results 2025 (16,92,794 appeared)

Three exams in 2025 show the scale. NEET UG: 22.8 lakh registered. CBSE Class 12: about 16.9 lakh students sat the exam, and that is one board out of many: ISC and the state boards add more. JEE Main: 15.4 lakh unique candidates. Every one of these includes physics. These groups overlap, so don’t add them up; but each on its own is a market of lakhs of students who need physics to make sense.
