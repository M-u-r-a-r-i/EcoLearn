The class’s top mix-up

A cricket ball skids across wet covers. 3 of 3 students who chose wrong said it “curves down, because its weight is an unbalanced force”.

The mix-up: forgetting the normal force from the ground, which balances the weight.

A score says “wrong”. EcoLearn tells the teacher which idea, who, and how sure they were.

Simulated data: our demo class, generated by script and run through the real app. Not pilot results.

Here is what a percentage hides. Students say how sure they are before every check. In this simulated class, “I’m sure” answers were right 91 percent of the time, “just guessing” 81. The interesting ones are the 8 answers that were sure but wrong: firmly held misconceptions, which the teacher sees by name. And because every wrong option maps to one misconception, the class page can say what the class actually believes: here, the three students who got the wet-covers question wrong all thought the ball curves down because its weight is unbalanced. They forgot the normal force. A score would only have said “wrong”.
