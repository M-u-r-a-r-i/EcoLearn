About a minute. Is this real, and can it work at scale? Why it is feasible: it is built on proven web technology and runs in any browser, on a phone or a laptop. Lessons are prepared once and reused by every student, so they load instantly and cost almost nothing to serve. We use AI only where it adds real value, and every AI part has a safe fallback, so the product keeps working even if the AI is slow or down. And we are not locked to one AI provider: changing the model is a setting, not a rebuild. How complete it is: the full student journey works, from first login to mastery. Teachers have a dashboard with AI lesson planning. The AI agents run live with safety checks. Privacy for minors is built in: no email addresses, and the AI never sees a student’s name. A student’s data can be exported or erased on request, and automated tests cover the whole system. The next step is hosting it for a school pilot with real classes.
