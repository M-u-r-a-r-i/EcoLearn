Today: Class 11 and 12 physics for the CBSE and ISC boards, in English

About a minute and a quarter. Our answer is EcoLearn, which fits the content to the learner instead of the other way round. First, it gets to know the learner: their interests, their hobbies, how they like to learn, and a five-minute starting check. Second, it teaches through their world: every concept explained through cricket, football, gaming, phones or motorsport, plus a short story built around their own hobby. Third, it finds the exact mix-up: every wrong answer points to the specific idea to fix, and their learning path adapts. Fourth, it keeps them going, with practice, revision at the right moment and progress they can see. And the teacher stays in the loop: they see where the class is stuck, and AI helps them plan what to do next.
