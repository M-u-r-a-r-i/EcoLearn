The four challenges of learning on your own

About a minute. Learning has changed. More and more of it happens alone: homework, revision, videos, apps, at the student’s own pace. That is a good thing: self-learning builds independence and works anytime, anywhere. But on your own, learning is fragile. Four things go wrong. Motivation fades, because nobody is nearby to keep you going when it gets hard. The content is one-size-fits-all: the same explanation whether it fits the way you think or not. Mistakes go unseen: nobody spots the wrong idea behind a wrong answer. And feedback comes late, often at the exam, when it is too late to fix the gap.
