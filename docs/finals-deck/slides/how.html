How EcoLearn works

Checked once in advance, personalised live

About a minute and a quarter. Two loops. The top one happens once, in advance, for every student. We start from the curriculum: the CBSE and ISC concepts, their teaching order, and the known mix-ups for each. A lesson is written for each concept in each interest, following a written guide, and every lesson is checked before any student sees it. The library then serves it instantly. The bottom loop is live, for one student. We retrieve their own lesson and their profile: their hobbies and the mix-ups from their starting check. An AI writer drafts a story around their hobby, grounded only in that lesson. Rules and a second AI check the physics, the analogy, where it breaks and its safety, and a failed draft is rewritten. Then the student gets the story, the lesson, a worked example and a check. If anything fails, they simply get the prepared story. Their answer to the check updates their plan, their mix-ups and their revision.
