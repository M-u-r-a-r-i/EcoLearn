Ideas they studied vs ideas they didn’t

Each student’s own unstudied ideas act as a rough control.

Studied: 18% to 73% (67 answers).
Not studied: 82% to 74% (101 answers).

Not proof: students study what they got wrong, so studied ideas start low. That is why the pilot is randomised.

Simulated data: our demo class, generated by script and run through the real app. Not pilot results.

EcoLearn also builds in a comparison. For every student we know which ideas they actually opened a lesson on and which they didn’t, so their unstudied ideas act as a rough control. In this simulated class, ideas students studied went from 18 to 73 percent right; ideas they didn’t study went from 82 to 74. We are careful about what this means: students study exactly the ideas they got wrong, so studied ideas start low and some rise is expected anyway. That is why our real pilot is a randomised comparison, written down before it starts.
