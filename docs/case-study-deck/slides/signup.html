Step 1 of 9 · real screen, sample student

Step one. Aarav’s teacher created his account. It is a username and a password, with no email and no phone number, because these are minors and we collect as little as we can. The first time he signs in, EcoLearn asks about him, not about physics: his hobbies and how well he knows them, how he likes to learn, how long he can focus, and how he feels about physics. It takes about four minutes, it is all taps, and any question can be skipped. There is no AI here: these answers feed plain rules that shape his lessons.
