Behind the screen: rules, no AI

Real screen · sample class (fictional students and numbers)

The Students tab puts the whole class on one screen: each student’s interest, how many concepts they have mastered, how long they actually read, how often they are right first time, and when they were last active. The teacher can move a student between Class 11 and Class 12, reset a forgotten password, and switch the AI parts off for any student whose family says no, in which case that student gets the prepared story and the lesson’s key idea instead. These names and numbers are our sample class.
