Now let’s spend a moment on the teacher, because this is where EcoLearn is most different. Setting up a class takes two minutes: paste the names and print login slips, no email or phone numbers. Then the teacher gets eight tools. Needs attention: who hasn’t started, who has gone quiet, who skims, who is sure but wrong. A mix-up map of what the class actually believes. Two AI helpers: a lesson plan for tomorrow, and a coaching note per student, each with the evidence behind it. Before and after checks. Full visibility of every AI step, with the power to hide any AI story. And privacy controls: switch AI off for one student, export or erase their data. The AI never sees a student’s name, only S1, S2, and the teacher always makes the decision.
