Source: UDISE+ 2021-22 to 2025-26, higher secondary (Classes 11-12), as analysed by educationforallinindia.com

Here is how many students are in Class 11 and 12 each year, from the government’s UDISE+ school census. It sits between 2.7 and 2.9 crore: 2.86 crore in 2021-22, dipping to 2.71, then back up to 2.89 crore in 2025-26, up 4.4 percent on the year before. The point for us: this is a steady market. A new Class 11 arrives every single year.
