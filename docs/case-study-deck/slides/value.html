So what can this reach? Start from the 2.89 crore students in Class 11 and 12. Between a third and almost half of them already pay for coaching at this level, roughly one crore students. Now look how thin 1 percent of all students is on this chart: 2.9 lakh students. At an assumed 100 rupees a month, that is about 35 crore rupees a year. Five percent is 173 crore; ten percent, 346 crore. The cost side is what makes it work: because lessons are written once per kind of learner and reused, the AI costs about 63 rupees per student a year in our model, around 5 percent of the price. These are estimates and assumptions, not results.
