Behind the screen: AI, checked by rules; rules note if it fails

Real screen · notes written by the AI (Gemini Flash-Lite) on the simulated class

Coaching notes are the second AI helper. For each student, an AI agent reads a small case file: how many lessons they opened, how they did, how long they read, a few of their survey answers, the ideas they mixed up. Never their name. It writes one short note: what the student did, and one thing to try, with the evidence shown as tags. If the AI breaks a rule or fails, that student gets a rules-based note instead. The best part: the suggestion can be a button. Here, “Add Newton’s third law to their revision list”. The teacher approves it, EcoLearn carries it out, and the follow-through line shows whether the student actually did it. Students never see these notes.
