Families are already paying for extra help. The government’s 2025 education survey found that at the higher secondary level, 44.6 percent of urban students and 33.1 percent of rural students take private coaching. Urban families spend about 9,950 rupees a year per student on it, rural families about 4,548. If EcoLearn charged 100 rupees a month, an assumption, not a decision, that is 1,200 rupees a year: an eighth of urban coaching, and a quarter of rural.
